    Questions

    Good to know.

    How long does it take?

    Wash and iron is ready in 48 hours. Hospital uniforms and white coats handed in by 10 am are ready the next morning. Dry cleaning takes 3 to 4 days.

    Do you pick up from the hostels?

    Yes. We collect from the TMCH hostel gates and the hospital gate at fixed times. Message us on WhatsApp to get the schedule.

    What if something gets lost or damaged?

    Every item is counted and tagged with your order number when we take it. If anything goes missing or gets damaged in our care, we'll make it right.

    Can you remove stains?

    We treat common stains like food, ink and turmeric. Tell us about any stain when you hand over the clothes. Some old stains may not come out fully.

    How do I pay?

    UPI or cash, at the shop or on delivery. Monthly plans are paid at the start of the month.
